76%

76% of 2025’s new demand for electricity was met by solar

Demand rose 855 TWh. Clean sources added 879 TWh, more than all of it, so fossil generation dipped slightly. One dot ≈ one percent of the year’s growth.

33.5%

33.5% from renewables, just ahead of coal’s 33.2%

The first time in 26 years of data. Coal’s share peaked at 40.9% in 2007.

Look inside “renewables”

Hydropower carries
the crossover

Without hydro, renewables are 19.6% of world power, far behind coal.

Wind and solar together are 17.3%. Hydro swings with rainfall, so a dry year can move the result.

How solid is the lead?

Renewables minus coal,

The gap closed over 15 years and only just turned positive. Ember’s latest year is often revised; a dry year like 2001 would also have undone it.
